# Roadmap

Planned work that isn't built yet. Items marked **User request** or **User-reported bug** came from Atelier users.

## Models and LoRAs

- **Import from Hugging Face links.** Paste a Hugging Face link to a model or LoRA and Atelier downloads and imports it directly, as Draw Things does.

## Canvas painting

Painting on the canvas, alongside masking:

- **Paint with colors.** Multiple colors and brush sizes. Select an area with Segment Anything, then fill it with a color.
- **Mask opacity.** Adjust how opaque the mask looks on the canvas. Display only: it doesn't change generation.
- **Sketch.** Draw on a white background or on top of an image.
